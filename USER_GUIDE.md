Welcome to the **Autonomous Enterprise Metamorphosis Engine (AEME) OS**.

## Part 1: The Core Philosophy
Before you begin clicking, you must understand the AEME paradigm:
- **Dashboards tell you what happened.** (Reactive)
- **AEME simulates what will happen and executes it.** (Proactive)

You are transitioning from an "Operator" to an "Orchestrator". Your primary job is now setting the strategic boundaries and letting the system optimize the execution.

## Part 2: Your First 24 Hours (Onboarding Workflow)

### Step 1: Initialization & Authentication
1. **Access the System:** Click "INITIALIZE SYSTEM" on the landing page (Make sure to open in a new tab if you are inside an iframe).
2. **Authenticate:** Sign in using an Enterprise Email or Google Workspace account.
3. **Configure Workspace Density:** At the top right of the dashboard, use the **Density Toggle** ("Compact View" vs "Comfort View") to adapt the application to your device. Use Comfort for touch displays and Compact for high information-density on a desktop setup.
4. **Database Spin-Up:** Once in the dashboard, locate the **Governance Control** panel (or **Memory Engine**) and click **"Run DB Migrations"**. This sets up your local execution tables, memory vectors, and audit logs.

### Step 2: Injecting Memory (The Context Layer)
AEME needs to understand your brand context before it can make intelligent decisions.
1. Go to the **Enterprise Memory Engine**.
2. Type in your brand's core positioning, past successful strategies, target audience demographics, or current macro-economic notes.
3. Click **"Ingest Memory Chunk"**.
   *Pro-tip:* Ingest snippets from your brand guidelines, competitor analysis, and past high-converting ad copy. AEME uses this to align its future PPC strategies with your brand voice.

### Step 3: Syncing Live PPC Data
1. Navigate to the **PPC Intelligence Engine**.
2. Click **"Sync Ad Networks"**. 
   *(In this v1, this simulates fetching raw data from Amazon Ads via an API).*
3. AEME will automatically ingest campaigns, calculate overall ROAS, and identify the highest-spending entities.

## Part 3: Running the Decision Engines

### The Simulation Engine (Testing the Future)
Never change a budget blindly.
1. Go to the **Simulation Engine**.
2. Input a hypothetical budget (e.g., $15,000).
3. The system runs a stochastic Monte Carlo-style simulation against your current ROAS metrics, factoring in diminishing returns based on market saturation.
4. Review the **Projected Revenue** and **Projected ROAS**. If the simulation yields a "Low" risk and high upside, you have found an optimization vector.

### The Strategic Chat System (Consulting the Oracle)
Sometimes, you need a high-level strategic pivot.
1. Open the **Strategic Chat System**.
2. Ask a complex question: *"How should we reallocate our budget given our current ROAS drop and the new memory context regarding our Q4 goals?"*
3. The system retrieves your specific memory embeddings, cross-references current PPC tables, and generates a structured JSON payload containing:
   - Deep Insights
   - The Reasoning
   - Concrete Execution Actions
   - Risk & Confidence levels

---

## Part 4: Achieving Autonomy (The Ultimate Goal)

You don't want to run simulations manually forever. You want the system to do it.

1. Go to the **Autonomous Control Plane**.
2. In production, this daemon runs on a CRON schedule (e.g., every hour). To test it manually, click **"Force Cycle"**.
3. **What happens behind the scenes?**
   - The engine observes all PPC metrics.
   - It identifies a severe inefficiency (e.g., a high-spend campaign dropping below target ROAS).
   - It formulates an action (e.g., `PAUSE_CAMPAIGN`).
   - It sends this action to the **Governance Layer** as a pending workflow.

---

## Part 5: The Governance Layer (Your Safety Net)

AEME will **never** execute a high-risk financial decision without your explicit approval.
1. After the Autonomy Engine proposes a workflow, check the **Governance Control** panel.
2. You will see a list of **Pending Approvals** (e.g., *"Scale 'SP - Core Products' by 20%"*).
3. Review the Risk Level (Green/Yellow/Red).
4. Click the checkmark to **Approve** or the X to **Reject**.
5. Every action is permanently recorded in the **Audit Log** for enterprise compliance.

## Expert Tips for Customization

1. **Adjusting Risk Tolerance:** If you find the Autonomy layer is too conservative, feed it a Memory Chunk stating: *"We are currently in a hyper-growth phase. Accept higher CAC for market share acquisition."* The Embeddings engine will pick this up during the next cycle.
2. **Reviewing the Audit Trail:** Make it a habit to check the Audit Log weekly. Ensure the AI's proposed decisions align with your long-term vision.
